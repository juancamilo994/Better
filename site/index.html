Better

Your site feels off. Better tells you why, and how to make your AI fix it.

Paste a URL. Better scores the design against a 10-criterion rubric on laptop and phone, shows what's weak with evidence, and turns the fixes you pick into one prompt for Cursor, Claude Code, Lovable or v0. Apply it, re-check, watch the score move.

See real resultsRun it yourself on GitHub
1. Paste a URLBetter reads the site and fills in who it's for and what it should do.
2. Get a scoreLaptop and phone screenshots, measured checks, and an AI judge score 10 criteria out of 100.
3. Pick fixesEach fix says what's wrong, where, and why it matters.
4. Copy the promptPaste it into your coding agent, then re-check and see what moved.

Real results

These are actual Better evaluations, recorded from the live engine. Nothing here is mocked.

Better is a hackathon project by Juan Camilo, Paul, Elle and Yitzy. Scores come from a versioned rubric (Hierarchy, Information density, Flow & effort, Interaction & feedback, Audience & tone fit, Originality, Visual communication, System consistency, Typography, Usability basics). Anything Better can't see from a still page is shown as "not assessed," never guessed. Source on GitHub.
